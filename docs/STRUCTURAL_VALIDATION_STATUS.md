# Structural solver validation status

Updated October 2026 with the rows of the returning `puremacro.trade` modules
(consistent accounting only, synthetic and OECD fixtures, the bundled OECD ICIO
2019 table), and on 23 September 2026 with a row for the stacked Newton-Krylov
and with the rows revised by the post-4.3.0 audit fixes. The 20 September 2026 update followed the
independent counterexamples of an internal critical evaluation and of its live
Dynare follow-up.
Feature availability, numerical convergence, and independent replication are
separate claims. Passing a residual check does not validate an economic model.

| Markov-switching DSGE | Coupled-equation residuals are recomputed at the returned matrices. Convergence is `diff <= tol` (absolute), or every residual entry within `tol` or within `rtol` (64 eps) of the magnitudes of its own cancelling terms (componentwise backward error; the smallest certifying `rtol` is reported as `relative_residual`). | Stacked-time Newton-Krylov | `solve_stacked_newton_krylov` accepts a path only when the stacked residual satisfies `|F|_inf <= tol * s` (s the steady-block scale) and the next Newton step is below `1e-10 * max(1, |Y|_inf)`; `step_tol=None` drops the step test (with `jacobian_scale=1.0` the test is then `|F|_inf <= tol`). The Newton move is bounded in path units (`0.75 * max(1, |Y|_inf)`) and accepted by a 24-trial Armijo line search. A direction returned at the Krylov cap is recorded (status and recomputed true linear residual) and line-searched like any other; it is not an error by itself. Failed line searches, iteration limits, errors or nonfinite values inside a linear solve, a residual at a noise floor that meets the tolerance while the step test cannot, and a steady-state stacked matrix that cannot be factored raise `StackedNewtonError` with the last state attached and the cause in `converged_by`. Block-Thomas pivots with a LAPACK condition estimate below `n * eps`, or a factorization whose one-vector backward error exceeds 1e-10, fall back to SuperLU (recorded in the preconditioner name). When both inputs are `StackedNewtonResult` objects, `compare_horizons` requires converged inputs, identical boundaries and the same exogenous path held beyond the short horizon; arrays, DataFrames and `PerfectForesightResult` inputs are compared as given. | Dense-solve oracles to 1e-12 (constant and per-date blocks, with and without time blocks), exact-inverse identity in one Krylov cycle, parity with `solve_perfect_foresight` on Ramsey and a 20-sector growth transition to 1e-8 (observed 7e-11 and 3.5e-11), equation-scaling invariance (1e-9) and variable-scaling invariance (units 1e-3 to 1e4, 1e-9 relative, same Newton counts), rounding-level and ill-conditioned pivot regressions (rank-deficient `B = U V`, stacked condition numbers 616 and 941; `B = [[1, 1], [1, 1 + 1e-12]]`), and the failure contract including errors inside the Krylov solve and a 1e-12 residual noise floor. Evidence covers analytic DSGE-size fixtures only. Memory is `O(T n^2)` (about 3.2 GB at `n_vars = 1000`, `T = 200`); systems with thousands of unknowns per date need a structured elimination, and none ships. Block structure alone does not establish speed or memory at scale, and the linear identities do not by themselves guarantee numerical accuracy or nonlinear convergence. No large-scale transition is solved here; MCP and the surprise mode of `solve_perfect_foresight` are unavailable. See [usage and evidence](dsge_stacked_newton.md). |

The 113-case validation gallery (16 subsystems, all passing) contains 62 internal,
31 analytical, 13 package, five SciPy and two published-reference cases, three of
them on the bundled trade table (world totals, column balance and the base
equilibrium); see [the validation gallery](VALIDATION.md). It has multiple tolerance classes.
Most trade evidence lives in the trade tests listed above, not in the gallery.
The gallery does not certify every feature, every backend or every deployment.

Puremacro ships no compiled extension of its own. NumPy/SciPy and other base
packages include compiled components; the mandatory dependencies also include
pandas, Matplotlib and requests. Pyodide importability, realistic browser
execution, GPU agreement and bitwise reproducibility require different tests.
